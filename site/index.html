DualPanto Web Firmware Uploader

Compile-free firmware upload for the DualPanto — pick a dualpantoframework branch, plug in the device, flash from the browser.

This browser does not support the Web Serial API.
Please use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or another Chromium-based browser (Safari and Firefox do not work).

1 Choose firmware version (branch)

2 Connect the DualPanto

Plug the DualPanto into a USB port, then click connect and pick its serial port (usually named CP2102 USB to UART Bridge Controller or SLAB_USBtoUART).

Hold the flash button! Press and hold the button on the back of the DualPanto (top left, next to the USB port) before clicking Upload and keep holding it until the log says flashing…. This puts the ESP32 into download mode — same as with the PyCharm workflow.

3 Compile-free upload

Troubleshooting
  • If connecting fails or no port shows up, install the CP210x driver and use a data-capable USB cable (not power-only).
  • If the log hangs at Connecting…, hold the flash button on the back of the device and try again.
  • Close PyCharm/VSCode serial monitors and any other tool using the port — the browser needs exclusive access.
  • If flashing fails at high speed, retry with baud rate 115200.
  • After a successful upload the device resets automatically. If handles behave strangely, press the reset button next to the power switch.
